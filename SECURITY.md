# Security Policy

## Averixa Group

At Averixa Group, we take the security of our technology, infrastructure, and digital services seriously.

We appreciate responsible security research and encourage the reporting of potential vulnerabilities that may affect our systems, applications, websites, or services.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a potential security vulnerability related to Averixa Group, please report it privately.

Email:

security@averixagroup.xyz

Please include, when possible:

- A description of the vulnerability
- The affected system, service, or URL
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Potential security impact
- Screenshots or technical details that may help us investigate

## Responsible Disclosure

Please do not publicly disclose a suspected vulnerability before Averixa Group has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address the issue.

We ask security researchers to:

- Avoid accessing or modifying data that does not belong to them
- Avoid disrupting our systems or services
- Avoid social engineering, phishing, or physical attacks
- Avoid automated testing that could negatively affect service availability
- Act in good faith when conducting security research

## Scope

This policy may apply to publicly accessible systems and services operated by Averixa Group.

Specific products or repositories may include additional security guidance.

## Response

We will make reasonable efforts to review legitimate security reports and determine appropriate remediation when necessary.
